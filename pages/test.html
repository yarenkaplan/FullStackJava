What Shapes Me

The things I take seriously outside of work.

I like having different areas in my life that challenge me in different ways. Some build discipline, some curiosity, and some simply help me stay present.

01

Fitness

Training is one of the clearest ways I practice consistency. It reminds me that progress usually comes from small efforts repeated over time.

02

Guitar

Playing guitar gives me a different kind of focus. It is both creative and technical, which is probably why I enjoy it so much.

03

Reading & Research

I enjoy reading beyond software and exploring ideas deeply. Research helps me understand not only how something works, but why.

04

Learning Languages

Learning a new language challenges both memory and perspective. It also helps me connect more naturally with different cultures and environments.

How I Think

A few principles I try to carry into everything I do.

Consistency

I trust steady progress more than short bursts of motivation.

Curiosity

I like understanding things deeply instead of stopping at the surface.

Discipline

I value showing up even when the process is repetitive or difficult.

Adaptability

New places, technologies and environments usually become opportunities to grow.

Still Learning

I want to keep becoming better at what I build and how I live.

For me, growth is not limited to a career path. It is a combination of learning deeply, staying curious, building discipline and continuing to explore new things.
